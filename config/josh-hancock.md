# Josh Hancock — Training Config

## Split

**Upper A · Lower A · Upper B · Lower B** — 4 days per week, rotating, each muscle group 2×/week.

| Day | Focus | Primary Muscles |
|---|---|---|
| Upper A | Chest, Back, Side Delts, Arms | Pecs, lats, rhomboids, side delts, triceps, biceps |
| Lower A | Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves | Full lower body |
| Upper B | Upper Chest, Back, Rear Delts, Arms | Upper pecs, lats, rear delts, triceps, biceps |
| Lower B | Hamstrings, Glutes, Unilateral, Calves | Posterior chain + unilateral stability |

## Schedule

Rotating — not tied to specific weekdays. Next session is always the next in rotation.

## Equipment

- **Force USA C10** — Smith machine, dual cable pulley (high + low), pull-up bar
- **Barbell + plates**
- **Adjustable dumbbells** — up to 27kg each
- **Adjustable bench** — flat + incline
- **Plate-loaded leg extension** — bench attachment
- **Leg curl attachment** — bench attachment
- **Preacher curl attachment** — bench attachment
- **Cable attachments** — lat bar, close-grip handle, rope, ankle strap, straight bar

## Current Goals

- Progressive overload on compound lifts
- Hypertrophy focus (8–12 rep range) on accessories
- Exercise rotation every 4–6 weeks to avoid complacency

## Progression Rules

- **Target reps hit across all sets** → +2.5kg next session (upper) / +5kg (lower)
- **Missed reps on final set only** → repeat weight
- **Missed reps on 2+ sets** → drop 5% next session, reassess
- **2 consecutive stalls** → swap exercise variation
- **DBs maxed at 27kg** → add reps (up to 15) then add sets (up to 4) before swapping to barbell variant
